ABOUT MINEIQ

Built for the people who keep mining moving.

MineIQ is a digital platform designed for mining engineers, mining professionals and students. It brings practical mining tools, engineering calculations, records, workforce management, equipment information, DGMS resources, career opportunities and AI assistance into one digital platform.

MineIQ is built around a simple idea: mining professionals should have useful technology available when and where they need it, whether they are working at a mine site, preparing for an examination or developing their professional skills.

OUR VISION

Building the future of digital mining.

Our vision is to create a smarter digital ecosystem for the mining industry, making technology, information and practical tools more accessible to mining professionals, engineers, students and organizations.

We believe digital technology can help make mining work more connected, organized, informed and efficient.
