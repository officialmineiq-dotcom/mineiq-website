About MineIQ

MineIQ is a digital platform created to support mining engineers, mining professionals and students in their everyday work, learning and career development.

Our goal is to bring useful mining technology, practical tools and information into one accessible digital assistant.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a smarter digital ecosystem for the mining industry, making technology more accessible to mining professionals, engineers, students and organizations.
